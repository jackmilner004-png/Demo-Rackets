# Photos

Drop images into this folder with these exact file names and the site picks them up
automatically. Until a file exists, that spot shows a striped grass-court background,
so the site never looks broken.

| File name            | Where it appears                         | Suggested shot                                   | Size (min)  |
|----------------------|------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|-------------|
| `hero.jpg`           | Full-screen landing banner               | Wide grass court, players mid-rally, clean lines | 2400×1600   |
| `centre-court.jpg`   | "The feel is everything" split section   | Show court from the stands, green & purple tones | 1600×1600   |
| `serve.jpg`          | Gallery (large tile)                     | Player serving, low angle                        | 1400×1800   |
| `net-play.jpg`       | Gallery                                  | Volley at the net                                | 1000×1000   |
| `ball-on-grass.jpg`  | Gallery                                  | Close-up of a ball on the baseline               | 1000×1000   |
| `crowd.jpg`          | Gallery (wide tile)                      | Spectators / scoreboard / ivy-covered walls      | 1800×1000   |
| `booking.jpg`        | Booking page banner + "Book" band        | Racket and balls on court, or a club court       | 2400×1200   |

Racket photos (optional): add them under `rackets/` and set `image:` on the racket
in `assets/js/data.js`, e.g. `image: "assets/photos/rackets/pro-staff-97.jpg"`.

## Image rights — please read

Photos of Wimbledon and other tour events are owned by the photographers/agencies
(e.g. AELTC, Getty Images, AP) and **cannot be used on a commercial website without
a licence**. Options:

- **Licence them** from Getty Images / AELTC / Shutterstock (editorial use).
- **Free-to-use stock** with a commercial licence: Unsplash or Pexels
  (search "grass court tennis", "tennis match", "tennis ball grass").
- **Your own photography** at your partner clubs — best for authenticity, and the
  racket stands themselves make great shots.

Also avoid using the Wimbledon name, logos or trademarks in a way that suggests
an official connection.

Keep each JPG under ~400 KB (export at ~80% quality) so the site loads fast on mobile.
