Our philosophy

The feel is everything.

A racket is personal. Weight, balance, string pattern — tiny differences change how every ball leaves the strings. The only way to know is to play.

Demo Rackets brings championship-grade equipment to the courts where you already play, so your next racket is chosen on court, not from a spec sheet.
